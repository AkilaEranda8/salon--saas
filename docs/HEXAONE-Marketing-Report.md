## 1. Executive summary

HEXAONE is an all-in-one, multi-tenant salon management SaaS built for modern salon businesses — from single-chair studios to multi-branch chains.

It combines:

- Day-to-day salon operations (appointments, walk-ins, payments, staff, inventory)
- Customer engagement (loyalty, offers, SMS, WhatsApp AI CRM)
- Growth tools (marketing automations, KPI dashboard, reviews)
- Business depth (accounting, commissions, multi-branch, white-label)

**Positioning line:**  
*Complete salon management platform — run the salon, grow the customer base, and control the business from one system.*

## 8. Competitive differentiation

1. **True multi-tenant SaaS** — each salon gets its own subdomain, branding, and plan entitlements  
2. **WhatsApp-native growth stack** — AI CRM + automations built in, not bolted on  
3. **Accounting depth** uncommon in “basic salon apps” (GL, VAT, payroll on Pro+)  
4. **Sri Lanka–ready GTM** — LKR, bank-slip culture, SL phone handling, local trust messaging  
5. **Staff + customer + owner triangle** — web, Hexaone staff APK, customer app, public booking, WordPress  
6. **Franchise / commission flexibility** — service-level and franchise commission modes  
7. **White-label path** — branding today, custom domain on Enterprise  
8. **Sales-operable platform** — impersonation, trial control, due-payment reminders, announcements

## 10. Go-to-market recommendations

### Acquisition

1. **Self-serve trial** via `admin.hexalyte.com/register`  
2. **Sales-assisted onboarding** via platform tenant create (preferred for paid deals — clean owner email login + welcome email + invoice)  
3. **Content & SEO** around “salon software Sri Lanka”, WhatsApp booking, commission tracking  
4. **Partner channel** — WordPress agencies embedding `[salon_booking]`  
5. **WhatsApp demos** — show live AI booking as the wow moment  

### Activation (first 7 days)

1. Create services + staff schedule  
2. Enable public booking link  
3. Connect WhatsApp (Pro) or at least SMS/email reminders  
4. Record first week of payments  
5. Invite staff to Hexaone APK
